Diff Reliability vs Availability?
Reliability measures whether the system has delivered the correct output at a specific, defined time—e.g., transferring payroll funds to the correct accounts on the right day. 
Availability measures the system’s up-time—for example, providing uninterrupted oxygen monitoring to premature babies during their necessary incubation period.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Scalability is the ability of a system to handle an increasing amount of workload without compromising performance

Association - Relation between two objects (Bank and Employees) - Represented by Simple Line
Aggregation - Has-A relation (Department and Students), (College and Students). Both can survive independently. 
Represented by hollow Diamond in main object side(who HAS the other objects). In this example, Department and College has that Diamond side.
Composition - Part-Of relation. The composed object can't exist without the other object. (Car and Engine) - An engine will not exist if car is destroyed/met with accident.
